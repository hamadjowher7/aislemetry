06 / CONTRIBUTOR QUESTIONSAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

Before the
first visit.

Is this a live task marketplace?+

No. These are sample tasks and app previews. Task availability, access permissions, approval policies and payment terms must be validated before launch.

Who can express interest?+

People interested in authorized local fieldwork can prepare a contributor profile. Eligibility, age requirements, training and smartphone specifications are not finalized.

What if a shop refuses photography?+

Decline the task and report that permission was refused. Never confront staff, enter restricted areas or continue unauthorized photography.

What happens if a photo is rejected?+

The proposed workflow would explain the issue, allow a suitable recapture or correction, and offer a review route. Review timelines, appeals and final payout rules are not yet operational.

Are the example rewards guaranteed?+

No. ₹55–₹120 figures are fictional task rewards, excluding travel, refusal, reshoot and review time. There are no live earnings or guaranteed payments.
