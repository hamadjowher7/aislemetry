05 / PARTNER QUESTIONSAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

The details
behind the work.

Is there current contracted work?+

No. The command center is a simulation of a proposed partner workflow. Campaigns, fees, workers, quality queues and invoices are fictional.

What separates agency work from individual tasks?+

Agencies manage batches, workforce allocation, internal QA, SLAs and invoice reconciliation. Individual contributors use a simpler task and wallet experience. Both require permissioned evidence.

Can our team connect an API?+

No production API or integration is claimed. The demo supports local CSV exports; a future API would require confirmed contracts, authentication and implementation.

How are disputed submissions handled?+

The proposed workflow records rejection reasons, correction evidence and adjustments before settlement. Actual commercial terms, dispute policy and payment cycles must be agreed before work begins.
