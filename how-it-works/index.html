01 / THREE PROCUREMENT MODESAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

The collection mode
changes the contract.

COMMISSIONED

Answer a defined buyer question.

Specific stores, categories, dates or contracted placements. Brand access and reuse follow the agreed scope; private evidence stays restricted by default.

SYNDICATED

Observe an independently defined panel.

Recurring, permissioned store selection. Eligible derived category insights may be licensed to multiple buyers, subject to rights and realized demand.

HYBRID

Keep the boundaries intact.

Additional category capture during a commissioned visit requires explicit store, supplier and customer permission. Shared content is separated from customer-restricted evidence.
