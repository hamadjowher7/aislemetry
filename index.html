11 / GOOD QUESTIONSAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

A clearer understanding.
Before a commitment.

Can a photograph tell us what is selling?+

It can show visible shelf presence at capture time. Sales, sell-through and stock turnover require POS, inventory or replenishment records. Shelf share is not sales market share.

Can we commission a bespoke audit?+

The envisioned commissioned workflow is scoped around your outlets, categories, cadence and evidence needs. The pilot builder lets you prepare a local proposal brief; no commercial service is booked.

What coverage is available today?+

The Bengaluru panel is entirely fictional. Actual cities, stores, permissioned access and delivery dates need validation during pilot scoping. No national coverage is claimed.

How does a recurring panel differ from a one-off audit?+

A matched panel revisits the same eligible stores with consistent capture framing and cadence. A commissioned audit can answer a specific placement question; permitted syndicated panels may support multiple buyer analyses.
