What is included in a proposal?+

A defined panel, sampling and replacement method, capture requirements, observation schedule, QA criteria, deliverables, rights and commercial terms. The browser brief is a preparation tool, not a proposal from a sales team.

Why is there no instant price?+

Actual costs depend on permissioned access, route density, collection complexity, review and recurrence. Publishing a fictional price would misrepresent commercial readiness.
