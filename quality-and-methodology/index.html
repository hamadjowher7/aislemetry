01 / THE SAMPLE DESIGNAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

Convenient collection
is not a representative panel.

Define the universe

Target city, zone, outlet format, category and eligibility. Listed outlets and permissioned stores are different counts.

Choose the panel

Use stated quotas or strata; record refusals, non-response, exclusions and replacements. Do not imply probability sampling without it.

Keep visits comparable

Return to the same eligible outlets with consistent framing and time-of-week. Track panel drift and independently recheck a subset.

04 / THE METRIC DICTIONARYAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

Make the definition
part of the number.

Share of visible facings+

Brand-visible facings ÷ total assessable category-visible facings, including Other / uncertain. A count is not sales share. The filtered denominator and accepted observations are shown in the explorer.

Planogram adherence+

Correct observed planned positions ÷ assessable planned positions, under the stated placement rule. Unassessable positions are reported separately and excluded, not treated as failures.

Priority SKU presence+

Visible planned target SKUs ÷ planned priority SKUs. This does not include stockroom inventory and does not prove a SKU is unavailable throughout the store.

Paid display coverage+

Verified target displays ÷ eligible sampled stores. It does not describe the full contract universe or every placement nationwide.

Matched-store change+

The difference between two observations of the same eligible store cohort, using stable category boundaries. Missing periods produce “insufficient history”, not a fabricated trend.

Observed shelf depletion+

Photographed changes can reflect purchases, replenishment, transfers, layout changes, theft, occlusion or angle. Photos alone cannot verify sales velocity, sell-through or causation.

06 / INDEPENDENCE AND LIMITATIONSAISLEMETRY / PRODUCT PREVIEW

Observe the shelf.
Don’t silently change it.

Observation and intervention are different tasks.

Paying a retailer to improve a display before measuring it changes the condition being observed. An independent observation program and a merchandising intervention need separate task types and labels.

Safeguards are an intended design.

Privacy obligations, retailer permission, labor classification, tax, retention and customer confidentiality require operational policies and legal review. This preview claims no certification, verified model accuracy or production compliance.
