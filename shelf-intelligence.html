What does a shelf intelligence analysis show?

A shelf intelligence analysis connects visible product identities, facing counts, and brand share of shelf to the shelf evidence. Aislemetry uses AI to support product detection and catalogue matching, with uncertain identities available for review. The walkthrough below uses a real supermarket photograph with five manually annotated packs to explain these outputs. It does not perform live AI recognition or report a full-category audit.

Unique SKU count
The number of distinct identified products, including their variant and pack size.
Visible facing count
The number of front-facing packs visible within the captured category.
Facings-based shelf share
Brand visible facings ÷ all visible category facings × 100.

FROM PHOTO TO REVIEWABLE EVIDENCE

Count the shelf.

01

Identify the exact SKU

Brand, product, variant, and pack size distinguish one SKU from another. A product catalogue with reference images helps define the expected range.

02

Separate SKUs from facings

Four identical packs side by side are one unique SKU and four facings. A different variant or pack size is a separate SKU.

03

Review uncertain matches

Small packs, blur, glare, and occlusion can make identity ambiguous. Keep uncertain products visible for review rather than treating every match as confirmed.

A shelf photo captures visible availability at a moment in time. It does not establish stockroom inventory, sales, or physical shelf dimensions without additional evidence.
SHELF ANALYSIS / ANSWERS

Understand the evidence.
Interpret the result.

Does this shelf example run AI on uploaded photos?+

No. This walkthrough uses a real supermarket photograph and five manually annotated packs. It animates the explanation of a shelf-analysis workflow. It does not upload photos, perform live image recognition, or report measured AI accuracy.

Why are five selected packs shown as one identified SKU and two review groups?+

The three selected Suntory bottles are manually grouped as one 680 ml barley-tea SKU using the packaging and shelf label. The two selected Healthworks drinks each need exact variant and size confirmation. They remain review groups and are excluded from the identified SKU count.

How is Suntory’s 60% share of the selected sample calculated?+

Suntory has three of the five manually selected packs. Three divided by five, multiplied by 100, equals 60%. This is the share of the marked teaching sample, not a measurement of the entire category or shelf. A full audit needs complete category coverage.

How should uncertain product identities be handled?+

Keep uncertain identities flagged for review and check the photo against the reference catalogue. Do not add an unresolved group to the confirmed SKU count. Keep its visible facings in the category total, and review brand shares before treating them as final.
