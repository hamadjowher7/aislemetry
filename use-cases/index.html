USE CASES / BUSINESS QUESTIONS WITH EVIDENCE

Start with the decision.
Work back to the shelf.

Different commercial teams need different observations. Define the measure, inspect its limits, and connect the finding to a next step.

01 / Trade marketing

Paid display verification

“Are the contracted facings and endcap placements actually present?”

Compare planned positions with observed placement, then inspect the 7 exception stores in the 25-store branded sample.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
02 / Category insights

Category shelf benchmarking

“How does visible category presence differ by store format?”

Compare shares across supermarket and grocery cohorts with fixed denominators, category boundaries and timestamps.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
03 / Regional sales

Priority SKU availability

“Which sampled stores are missing visible priority products?”

Review SKU presence with full-category evidence; distinguish an absent photographed item from whole-store stockout.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
04 / Revenue growth teams

Price and promotion checks

“What shelf-label prices and offers were observable?”

Inspect readable labels, promo markers and dates. Unknown labels are unavailable, not zero-priced.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
05 / Brand insights

Repeat-panel change

“Where did our visible shelf presence change over four visits?”

Use the same 113 assessable stores, not a changing convenience sample. Inspect loss cases before choosing an intervention.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
06 / Retail experience teams

Permissioned mystery audits

“Can consistent observations answer a service or execution question?”

Define a structured question set, eligible store scope, required evidence and review rules without private or unauthorized photography.

Explore the sample workflow ↗
07 / Launch teams

New-product launch monitoring

“Are new SKUs visible in target stores over repeated visits?”

Set a launch-specific expected range and compare presence and promotional execution at a consistent cadence.
